Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What types of caravans do you offer?
We offer luxury caravans, family cruisers, desert explorers, executive mobile offices, and presidential suites. Each model can be customized to your specific requirements.
Do you provide custom furniture solutions?
Yes, we specialize in custom furniture for villas, hotels, offices, and restaurants. Our team works closely with you to design and manufacture pieces that match your vision.
What is the typical delivery time?
Delivery times vary by product. Standard caravans take 45-60 days, custom furniture 10-15 days, and exhibition stands 10-20 days. We always strive to meet your timeline.
Do you offer warranty on your products?
Yes, all our products come with comprehensive warranty coverage. Caravans have up to 5-year warranty, furniture up to 5 years, and stands up to 2 years depending on the product.
Are your products certified?
Absolutely! We are ISO 9001:2015 certified and our products meet SASO (Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization) standards for quality and safety.
Do you provide international shipping?
Yes, we ship our products worldwide. We have a dedicated logistics team to ensure safe and timely delivery to any location.
